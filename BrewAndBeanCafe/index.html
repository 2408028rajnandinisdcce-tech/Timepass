Our Story

A Love Letter to Good Coffee

What started as a tiny kiosk in 2014 has grown into the neighbourhood's favourite spot. We source single-origin beans from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, roast them in small batches, and brew each cup with care.

We believe great coffee does not need to be complicated - just honest, thoughtful, and made by people who genuinely love what they do.
